About this product

The Desbro SMART STAPLER™ is a surgical skin stapler used to close skin incisions quickly and evenly, as an alternative to sutures where a clinician considers it suitable.

It is manufactured in our licensed facility in Hyderabad and supplied to hospitals, surgical centres and clinics, directly and through our distributor network.

  • Designed for quick, even skin closure
  • Easy-grip handle
  • Use with the Desbro Skin Staple Remover for removal
