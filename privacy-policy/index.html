Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Desbro Biosciences Private Limited (“Desbro”, “we”) runs desbrobiosciences.com. This page explains what information we receive through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

This website has no forms, user accounts or online payments. We only receive information you choose to send us when you call, email or message us on WhatsApp, for example your name, company, phone number, email address and the details of your enquiry.

How we use it

We use it only to reply to your enquiry, send quotations, samples and product information, and process and deliver orders. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Services this website uses

  • Hosting: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security and operation.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (Google LLC) collects website usage statistics, as described below.
  • Fonts: text fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers.
  • WhatsApp: if you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy also applies.

Website statistics: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use this website, for example which pages are viewed, roughly which city or country visits come from (based on IP address), which website referred the visit, and whether the call, email or WhatsApp buttons were used or the email address was copied. Google Analytics sets cookies for this. We do not use advertising cookies and do not use this data to identify individual visitors. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

WhatsApp messages and emails: when you tap a WhatsApp or email button, the pre-filled WhatsApp message or email subject includes a short reference (for example "ref: GOOGLE · stapler") showing how you reached the website. You can delete it before sending.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal, tax and regulatory record-keeping obligations as a medical device manufacturer.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you have shared with us, subject to the records we must keep by law. Write to info@desbrobiosciences.com.
