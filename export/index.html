Frequently asked questions

Which countries do you export to?

We export to the African market, currently to Kenya. We welcome enquiries from importers and distributors in other markets. Tell us your country and requirements.

Can we get samples before ordering?

Yes. Samples are available on request, along with the document set for evaluation.

What documents can you provide to register the product in our country?

Copies of our ISO 13485:2016 certificate, Form MD-5 manufacturing licence and endorsement, and a Free Sale Certificate. A certificate of analysis and a sterilisation certificate are sent with every stapler batch.

Can you supply under our own brand?

Yes. We print your brand and logo on the device and your artwork on the pouch, box and carton. Manufacturer details stay on the label as the regulations require.

What is the HS code for these products?

9018 90 29, for both the skin stapler and the skin staple remover.

How are prices and delivery terms quoted?

Prices, payment and delivery terms are quoted separately, per order and destination. Send us your market and the volumes you have in mind and we will reply with a specification sheet and a quotation.
